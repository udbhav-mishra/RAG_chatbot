The Ramayana is one of the two great Sanskrit epics of India (the other being the Mahabharata). Traditionally attributed to Valmiki, it explains the life of Rama and explores duty (dharma), kingship, family, loyalty, and morality. Rather than a dramatic retelling, here is a knowledge-based overview with major events and important characters.

1. Background of the World

The Ramayana takes place in the ancient kingdom of Kosala with its capital at Ayodhya.

The gods believed evil forces led by the demon king Ravana had become powerful. Ravana had obtained blessings making him difficult to defeat. Since he underestimated humans, the gods decided that Vishnu would incarnate as a human prince: Rama.

2. King Dasharatha and His Family

King Dasharatha ruled Ayodhya.

His three queens:

Kaushalya — mother of Rama
Kaikeyi — mother of Bharata
Sumitra — mother of Lakshmana and Shatrughna

Their sons:

Rama
Bharata
Lakshmana
Shatrughna

Rama was considered ideal in behavior and leadership.

3. Sage Vishwamitra and Early Adventures

Sage Vishwamitra requested Rama and Lakshmana to protect sacred rituals from demons.

Important characters introduced:

Tataka — killed by Rama
Maricha — later important in Sita's abduction
Subahu — defeated

This phase established Rama as a warrior.

4. Sita and Marriage

In the kingdom of Mithila ruled by King Janaka:

Major characters:

Sita — daughter of Janaka
Urmila
Mandavi
Shrutakirti

Janaka organized a contest involving the bow of Shiva. Rama lifted and broke it, winning Sita's hand.

The four brothers married the four princesses.

5. Kaikeyi's Boons and Exile

Dasharatha planned Rama's coronation.

Kaikeyi's maid:

Manthara

Manthara convinced Kaikeyi to demand two previously promised boons:

Bharata should become king
Rama should go into exile for 14 years

Dasharatha reluctantly agreed.

Characters accompanying Rama:

Sita
Lakshmana

Dasharatha later died from grief.

Bharata rejected the throne and ruled symbolically with Rama's sandals representing Rama's authority.

6. Forest Period and Important Encounters

Rama encountered many sages and beings.

Notable characters:

Ahalya
Agastya
Shabari
Jatayu

Demons encountered:

Khara
Dushana

Most important:

Shurpanakha attempted to approach Rama and Lakshmana. After being insulted and injured, she informed Ravana.

This directly led to Sita's kidnapping.

7. Sita's Abduction

Ravana devised a plan.

Key participants:

Ravana
Maricha
Jatayu

Maricha transformed into a golden deer to distract Rama.

Lakshmana briefly left Sita after hearing Rama's voice.

Ravana disguised himself as a sage and abducted Sita.

Jatayu attempted rescue but was defeated.

Ravana took Sita to Lanka.

8. Lanka and Ravana's Family

Ravana ruled Lanka.

Important family members:

Mandodari — Ravana's wife
Vibhishana — righteous brother
Kumbhakarna — giant warrior
Indrajit
Akshayakumara
Trijata

Sita remained imprisoned in the Ashoka grove and refused Ravana's proposals.

9. Alliance with the Vanaras

Rama met monkey allies.

Key characters:

Hanuman
Sugriva
Vali
Angada
Jambavan
Nala
Nila

Rama helped Sugriva regain power by defeating Vali.

Sugriva then helped search for Sita.

10. Hanuman's Mission

Hanuman became central.

Events:

Jumped across the ocean
Entered Lanka
Found Sita
Gave Rama's ring
Defeated warriors
Burned parts of Lanka
Returned with information

This confirmed Sita's location.

11. Construction of Rama Setu

Under Nala and Nila's leadership, an army bridge called:

Rama Setu

was built to reach Lanka.

12. The Great War

Major warriors on Rama's side:

Rama
Lakshmana
Hanuman
Sugriva
Angada
Jambavan
Vibhishana

Major warriors on Ravana's side:

Ravana
Indrajit
Kumbhakarna
Atikaya
Prahasta
Nikumbha

Important events:

Indrajit used magical weapons
Lakshmana defeated Indrajit
Kumbhakarna entered battle and died
Hanuman carried a mountain containing medicinal herbs after advice from Sushena
Rama eventually killed Ravana

Vibhishana became king of Lanka.

13. Return to Ayodhya

Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana returned after 14 years.

This return is associated with the festival:

Diwali

Rama was crowned king.

This period is called Rama Rajya—an ideal kingdom representing justice and prosperity.

14. Later Events (Uttara Kanda Tradition)

Some versions include later events:

Due to public doubts about Sita, Rama sent her to the forest.

There she lived in the hermitage of Valmiki.

Children:

Lava
Kusha

Lava and Kusha later met Rama and recited the Ramayana.

Eventually Sita returned to Mother Earth.

Rama later ended his earthly life and returned to his divine form.
